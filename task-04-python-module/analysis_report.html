Sneaker Sales Analysis Report

1. Dataset Overview

Explanation: In this step, we examined the dataset's structural integrity, column types, and overall size to ensure the data is suitable for analysis.
Conclusion: The dataset contains 5000 rows and 19 original columns. All data types are correct, and an additional 'month' column was successfully added for trend tracking.

2. Data Quality Analysis

Explanation: We performed a quality check using Box Plots to identify numerical anomalies in shoe sizes, prices, and customer birth years. We also checked for missing values and duplicates.
Conclusion: The data is clean. No missing values or duplicates were found. Outlier analysis confirms that shoe sizes (35-47) and prices are within realistic market ranges.

3. Brand Popularity

Explanation: We analyzed the frequency of transactions for each sneaker brand to determine which brands have the highest sales volume.
Conclusion: There are 7 unique brands. Reebok and Under Armour are the most frequently sold, showing high market turnover.

4. Financial Performance

Explanation: We calculated the total revenue generated by each brand and visualized the monthly sales dynamics to identify trends and financial leaders.
Conclusion: Nike is the revenue leader. The monthly dynamics show consistent sales throughout the year, indicating a stable business model with no significant seasonal drops.
